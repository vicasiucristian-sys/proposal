## Founder's decisions (keep these unless the founder changes them)

- Sells **only AI video ads + ad scripts** to D2C / e-commerce brands. **No cinematic or film work** in this studio (Velrun, the founder's other brand, does commercials and short films).
- Based in **Romania**. Sell first to English-speaking brands in the US, UK and Western Europe; local (Romanian / Eastern European) prices exist too.
- Production is **automated with AI agents**. Founder's numbers: **2–3 hours per ad start to finish, 5 ads run in parallel** (≈ 30 minutes of founder time per ad), **AI/API cost up to $15 per ad** (ads up to 60 s). The founder says the output is high quality, not template spam: treat automation as an advantage (speed, more concepts, margin), never as "cheap automated ads".
- Separate studio name. Mentioning Velrun is optional.
- Open questions: which market first, existing D2C case studies, studio name, company registration status.

## Short answer

- Demand is real: Upwork freelancer earnings for AI video generation/editing +329% in 2025; Fiverr searches for "AI UGC video ads" +265% (Nov 2025–Apr 2026).
- Simple AI production is getting cheap: done-for-you 30 AI videos for $499 (Shhots); a Shopify app sells 120 AI UGC ads for $299/mo (ADGPT); automated pipeline templates are public; Upwork earnings per contract for simple generative/creative work −13% (Jul 2026). Sell ideas, scripts, testing and quality, not video files.
- Room in the middle ($500–$4,000/mo): few sell strategy + scripts + ads together. None of 25+ companies studied sells a scripts-only plan or a paid test batch, publishes a quality checklist, or includes compliance.
- With the pipeline, alone ≈ 5 clients ≈ $18,600/mo sales, ≈ $16,000 profit before tax (est.). The bottleneck is sales, scripts and client work.
- International clients pay ~2× local prices.
- Rules: fake AI "customers" are illegal (US, UK, EU); label realistic AI people (New York since 9 Jun 2026, EU since 2 Aug 2026); TikTok requires AI disclosure; Google AI label setting since Jul 2026.
- Romania: SRL micro-enterprise pays 1% of revenue under €100k (needs a minimum-wage contract); above that 16% profit tax + 16% dividends.

## Demand

- IAB 2026: 62% of video ad buyers use generative AI (51% in 2025); 33% of video ads use it (43% expected 2027); 96% of small/mid buyers unhappy with their AI use; top wish: proof it performs (46%).
- Meta Andromeda (Dec 2024) rewards different concepts; near-copies get grouped. Ads wear out in 2–4 weeks. TikTok: add 3–5 new creatives about every 7 days. Only ~4–8% of ads become winners.
- Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026, new ads per month (avg / top 25%): <$10k spend 12/21; $10–50k 18/35; $50–200k 29/69; $200k–1M 49/135; $1M+ 82/237.
- NYU/Emory field study (Dec 2025): fully AI-made ads up to +19% CTR vs human-made; saying "made with AI" cut the effect up to 31.5%. Lab studies: human presenters win on trust and purchase intent. One agency owner reported AI ads with clicks but no sales; real testimonials made $20,000 (self-reported). Offer a mix: AI for hooks/scenes/product shots, brand's real customers for testimonials.
- AI video generator market: $788.5M (2025) → $3.44B by 2033 (Grand View Research).
- Meta: 9M+ small businesses use its AI creative tools; it turns up to 20 product photos into an ad. Simple product videos will become free.

## Ideal client

- Shopify / D2C brands, ~$1–20M revenue (est.), $10k–$200k/mo on Meta (often TikTok), 1–5 marketers, no video team, launching 4–7 new ads a week.
- Buying triggers: ad fatigue, Meta price per ad +12% YoY (Q1 2026), launches, Black Friday, new countries/languages, slow or expensive UGC creators, a job post for an "AI video editor" (warm lead).
- Categories: start with home & decor, pet, food & drink, accessories, gifts, lifestyle beauty. Careful: fashion, jewelry, skincare. Avoid at first: supplements, weight loss, skincare results, CBD, alcohol, finance, kids, products needing a real demo.
- Second client type: media-buying agencies buying white-label (pay ~$1,200/client, resell $2,000–$3,000).

What to copy: tier cards with ads/month (Admiral); explained steps and "product never altered" (Webaes); speed promise from one product photo (Unreal Media Group, 72 h); month-to-month + first-month money back (Top Growth Marketing); pricing and competitor-comparison pages for search (Shhots); tiers tied to client ad spend and a weekly results loop (Darkroom); case studies with CPA/ROAS and the message change (inBeat, Hurom −65% CPA); cheap paid trial (Vidpros $100); money-back window (Video Husky 14 days).

Competitor weak spots to turn into promises: hidden prices/credits → public prices; lock-in → month to month after 3 months, 30-day rollover; hard to cancel/slow revisions → cancel by email, revisions within 2 business days; changing staff → same person + brand memory doc; UGC creators ignore script → AI follows the approved script; extra fees → captions, formats, variants included; fear of bad AI → published checklist, labels, no fake testimonials.

Gaps: price middle; scripts-only plan; paid test; published quality checklist; compliance included; D2C proof (competitors show app results); monthly learning report; checked European languages ($1–$2.50 per version).

## Pipeline economics

| Deliverable | Founder time | AI cost |
|---|---|---|
| Script with 3 hooks (agent drafts, founder edits) | ~30 min (1 h from scratch) | < $0.30 |

| Hook variant | ~3 min | $0.30–$2.50 |
| Extra format re-render | ~1 min | $0.10–$0.20 |
| Language version | ~6 min + native check | $1–$2.50 |
| Client work | 4–12 h/month per client | – |

Research on expert-run pipelines: 22–50 human minutes per ad (vs 4–6 h by hand); API cost $2.60–$8.20 per 25–30 s avatar ad, $7.30–$13.80 fully generative.

Human approval gates: onboarding (brand kit, packshots, claims + proof, banned words, consented presenters) → brief approval (concept matrix, ensure variety) → script + claims approval → keyframe/product-fidelity check on stills → agents generate and edit → founder watches every master ad, spot-checks variants → client approves and launches → weekly 30-min learning review.

Lean stack (fixed ≈ $240–$380/mo + usage): n8n self-hosted (~$7), Claude API, Airtable 2 seats (~$48), HeyGen API / Creatify API ($0–$99), Gemini API (Veo, Nano Banana Pro) + fal.ai (Kling, Seedance) + Runway API, ElevenLabs Creator (~$22) + sync.so, Creatomate $129 / Shotstack $39 / Remotion (free ≤3 people), Frame.io 2 seats (~$30). Limits: HeyGen ~10 concurrent jobs; Veo ~50 requests/min. Creatomate raised prices in 2026; HeyGen ended free API credits; Sora shut down (app Apr 2026, API 24 Sep 2026).

Quality checklist (publish it): product/label/logo/colors match packshot, real packshot on close-ups and end card; hands/eyes/teeth/lip sync; continuity of face, clothes, product; captions from approved script + brand word list; specs (hook in 3 s, 9:16 safe zones, length, loudness, naming); only approved claims, no "verified buyer" avatars; licensed presenters/voices/music, written consent for real people; AI labels + platform settings, keep AI metadata; concept variety; records of prompts, tools, approvals.

## Scripts

- Fiverr scripts $35–$40; professionals $150–$800; one supplement brand pays $400/test script + $500–$1,000 per winner.
- Each script: header (goal, customer, awareness stage, angle, offer, claims + proof), 3 hooks from different angles, body + CTA, VO, on-screen text, shot list (3–8 s shots, real product photos), 15 s and 30 s versions.
- Winning formats (Motion, 550k+ ads): offer-first, demo, testimonial, unboxing, montage, listicle, split-screen, us-vs-them, before/after (risky in health/beauty). Hooks at $1M+/mo accounts: confession, bold claim, relatability, contrast, curiosity. Hook rate ≥30% good; hold rate ~25%; ~5% of ads win.
- Lengths: Meta Reels ≤15 s cold / 15–30 s demos, 9:16, text out of bottom 35%; Feed 4:5; TikTok 21–34 s; YouTube Shorts < 60 s.
- Monthly loop: research (reviews, Reddit, Amazon, TikTok comments, Meta Ad Library) → angle map → agent drafts, founder sharpens → produce after approval → test 3–7 days → next batch 60% winner variations / 30% new formats / 10% new ideas → monthly memo.

## Rules (checked Sep 2026)

- US FTC reviews & testimonials rule (21 Oct 2024): no testimonials from people who don't exist (AI included); fines >$50,000 per violation.
- UK DMCC Act (6 Apr 2025) bans fake reviews; UK ASA: an AI label can't fix a misleading before/after (May 2025).
- Meta (1 Jun 2026): auto-detects outside-AI media, shows "AI info"; self-disclosure required only for political/social ads; don't strip AI metadata.
- New York GBL §396-b (9 Jun 2026): conspicuous disclosure of "synthetic performers" in ads; $1,000 then $5,000 per violation; brand and producing studio exposed.
- Google Ads (9 Jul 2026): AI label for commercial ads (in creative or "AI label" setting).
- EU AI Act Art. 50 (2 Aug 2026): realistic AI people = deepfakes, must be labeled; fines up to €15M / 3%; Digital Omnibus only gave tool providers until 2 Dec 2026 for machine-readable marking. The studio that decides AI use is usually the one that must label.
- TikTok: AI-generated ads need the "AI-generated content" disclaimer or own label; no real person's likeness in AI endorsements.
- Do: presenter framing, not customer testimonials; keep proof and permission for any real review repeated by an AI presenter and say "AI presenter"; licensed presenters/voices; client sign-off on claims; plans matching revenue (e.g. Midjourney Pro above $1M revenue); no free plans for client work.
- Don't: invent stories/ratings; AI doctors/experts; AI before/after; lookalikes or cloned voices without consent; promise copyright or better CPA than human UGC; other brands' IP or living artists' styles; "Are you struggling with…?" lines.
- Client contract: scope, revisions, delivery from approved script, payment/term/pause, rights "to the extent rights exist", licensed assets, client promises on materials/claims/reviews, who labels AI, no performance guarantee, liability cap + mutual indemnities.

## Romania setup (2026, confirm with an accountant)

- SRL micro: 1% of revenue (3% rate abolished by OUG 89/2025) under €100,000 (~509,630–509,850 lei for 2026); needs ≥1 employee or a mandate contract at minimum wage (4,325 lei gross from 1 Jul 2026; employer cost ~4,418 lei); 90 days after registration (OUG 8/2026). Share capital 500 lei (5,000 lei once turnover > 400,000 lei).
- SRL profit tax 16%; dividends 16% + health contribution 10% on a fixed band (max 9,720 lei). Profit can be retained at 16%.
- PFA: 10% income tax; CAS 25% on 12 or 24 minimum wages; CASS 10% capped at 72 minimum wages; unlimited personal liability.
- What you keep (est., €1 = 5.10 lei, costs 15%): at €60k: micro €38.2k, SRL profit €34.1k, PFA €37.0k; at €200k: SRL profit €118.0k, PFA €143.6k (micro not allowed). Recommendation: SRL for liability protection; discuss PFA's tax advantage vs risk.
- VAT: 21% standard; registration threshold 395,000 lei (~€77k), sources disagree whether services sold abroad count. EU B2B clients: reverse charge, VIES check, Art. 317 VAT code before first invoice if not registered, D390 monthly. UK/US B2B: not taxable in Romania. Buying US/EU AI tools: 21% reverse-charge VAT paid via D301 even if not registered (Art. 317 code for EU suppliers); voluntary full registration makes it neutral. Put the RO VAT code in vendor billing.
- Payments: Stripe works for Romanian companies (~1.5% + €0.25 EEA cards, 2.5% UK, 3.25% international, +~2% FX; Billing +0.7%); Wise Business (local USD/GBP/EUR accounts, FX from ~0.33%); Payoneer; PayPal worst for B2B. US clients: W-8BEN-E; services performed outside the US normally have no US withholding.
- Contracts: MSA + order form; templates Common Paper PSA, Bonterms PSA; governing law: Romanian/client law for EU, English law for UK, arbitration or client's state law for US.
- Insurance: professional liability from ~€250–300/yr (Groupama, Omniasig, Signal Iduna); confirm AI-content claims are covered (US insurers adding generative-AI exclusions from 2026).
- Trademark: EUIPO €850 for one class (SME Fund may refund part); check name at ONRC and TMview.
- Ask the accountant: micro vs profit-tax SRL if >€100k in year 1; full VAT registration vs Art. 317 code; whether exported services count toward the VAT threshold; withholding on foreign payments for music/voices/stock; multi-currency invoicing; pending 2026–27 changes.

## Risks

Price pressure from cheap AI services and Meta's tools → sell ideas, scripts, testing, quality; niche down. Automation is copyable → product accuracy, concept variety, claims control, checklist, learning loop. Lower trust in AI presenters → mix with real customer videos, honest labels, test labeled AI vs human UGC. Legal (fake testimonials, missing labels) → rules + contract. Tool shutdowns/price/API limits → 2–3 vendors per step, queues, fallbacks, monthly cost review. Quality at volume → checklist, human watch of every master, stills approved first. Not enough clients → daily sales, salesperson at 3–4 clients. Early churn → 3-month minimum, a winner in 30 days, day-30 review, monthly reports, pause option.
